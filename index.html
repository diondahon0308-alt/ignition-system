Bonds draws a line between every bound pair. weak medium strong (g-rich).
Coordination colours each Prima by how many neighbours it is bound to: dark blue = loose, gold = tightly bound.
Layers halos each Prima by depth in its structure: corebodyedge
Phase colours each Prima by its emergent state: solid compact, liquid spaced, gas widely spaced. Flow map averages Prima motion into arrows (blue → orange by speed) and adds thin orange heat-flow arrows, so you can watch a Cyclone's insides spin and see where energy travels. Light hides the Primas and shows only what light reveals: lit Je+Ky compounds (more Je → shorter wavelength: red → violet), moving light rays, and the Primas those lights fall on. Try a Star or a Jeg-Ky Onogon. Turn the Primas layer off to keep cells as solid bodies with their rings.
Compound bonds are always shown as white springs. Use 🔍 Inspect on a structure to see its composition and cohesion.
Onogons (Prima compounds) have three states: Rigid fully bonded and anchored, Loose partly bonded or weakly bonded, Mixed components present but not bonded. Mixed → Loose → Rigid as bonds form; heat above +2,600 °U breaks them. Light from Je+Ky needs a bond: Rigid glows fully, Loose dimly, Mixed not at all.
Envelopes are compounds of compounds. They survive some vaporized structures and keep a trace of their history; use 🔍 Inspect on one to read it.
